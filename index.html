The Unfiled Archive

The Unfiled Archive is a channel of short illustrated videos about little-known stories from history.

We use a private desktop tool to schedule and upload our own videos to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. The tool signs in with TikTok Login Kit and uses the Content Posting API only to upload videos that we created, to the account that authorised it. It does not read, collect or publish anything from other users.
