Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how "The Unfiled Archive" desktop application (the "App") handles data.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload videos to the connected account.
  • Upload and publish status of the videos we send.

2. How we use it

Only to upload and schedule the account owner's own videos. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising or profiling.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the account owner's computer. We do not run servers that collect user data.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. You can revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services; the local token file is then deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
